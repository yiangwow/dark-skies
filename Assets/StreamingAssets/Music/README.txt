Drop your tracks here (.ogg or .wav) and they become the game's soundtrack.

- Streamed at runtime: no Unity rebuild needed, nothing gets committed.
- Shuffled playlist, loops forever.
- The mix is dynamic: combat opens the low-pass filter and volume,
  quiet stretches muffle down, round end ducks everything.
- In a built player the folder lives at AlienSkies_Data/StreamingAssets/Music.

Breakcore encouraged.
